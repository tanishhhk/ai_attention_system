In short

Focus is a demonstration project. Its score is an estimate from a few hand-written rules about head, eye and shoulder positions. Don't use it to make decisions about your health, your safety or other people.

A demonstration, not a product

This site is a personal portfolio project by Tanishk Jain, built to show a computer-vision technique. It is not a finished or supported product, and it may change or go offline at any time.

Don't rely on it to judge whether you are alert enough to drive, operate machinery or do anything else where a mistake could cause harm.

Not for monitoring other people

Don't use this site, or code from it, to watch or score anyone without their informed consent. Its scores are not suitable for decisions about people, such as grades, exams, work performance, hiring or discipline.

Accuracy and limits

  • The score comes from rules, not a trained model. It measures where your head, eyes, mouth and shoulders are, not what you are thinking about.
  • Lighting, glasses, hair, camera angle, camera quality and how you sit can all move the detected points and change the result.
  • The slouch limit was set by measuring 20 AI-generated example photos. The other limits are starting values. None have been tested across many real people.
  • Results can be wrong in either direction: flagging problems that aren't there, or missing ones that are.

AI-generated example photos

The people in the examples were generated with AI (Google Gemini) to avoid using anyone's real face. They are not real, and any resemblance to a real person is unintended. Generated faces may not show expressions the way real faces do, so the live check with your own camera is the better guide to how the app behaves.
