How focused do you look right now?

This page watches your posture and face through your webcam and scores your attention from 0 to 100, using seven plain rules you can read below. It all runs in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

How it works

From a camera frame to a score, in five steps.

  1. Find the pointsMediaPipe finds 33 body points and 478 face points in each frame.
  2. MeasureEighteen of them become six ratios, so camera size and distance don't matter.
  3. Take off pointsEach ratio past its limit takes points off 100. Any one problem leaves focused.
  4. Watch for yawnsTwo yawns within a minute take off 20 more, even between yawns.
  5. Label itAbove 80 focused, above 60 attentive, above 40 low attention, above 20 distracted.

Limits

These are hand-written rules, not a trained model, and they measure where your head, eyes and shoulders are, not what you're thinking. Lighting, glasses and camera angle all move the points. The slouch limit comes from measuring the example photos; the others are starting values, untested across many real people.
