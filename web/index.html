How focused do you look right now?

This page watches your posture and face through your webcam and scores your attention from 0 to 100, using six plain rules you can read below. It all runs in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

Each example is a set of landmark positions, the same data the camera produces, scored by the same rules as the live check.

How it works

  1. MediaPipe finds 33 body points and 478 face points in each video frame.
  2. Three body points and fifteen face points become six measurements. Each is one distance divided by another in the same frame, so camera resolution and how close you sit don't change the result.
  3. Every measurement past its limit takes points off a starting score of 100.
  4. The score sets the state: above 80 is focused, above 60 attentive, above 40 low attention, above 20 distracted, otherwise critical.

Limits

These are hand-written rules, not a trained model. Lighting, glasses and camera angle all move the landmarks, and the limits are starting values that haven't been tuned across many people. It measures where your head, eyes and shoulders are, not what you're thinking about.
