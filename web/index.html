How focused do you look right now?

This page watches your posture and face through your webcam and scores your attention from 0 to 100, using seven plain rules you can read below. It all runs in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

How it works

  1. MediaPipe finds 33 body points and 478 face points in each video frame.
  2. Three body points and fifteen face points become six measurements. Each is one distance divided by another in the same frame, so camera resolution and how close you sit don't change the result.
  3. Every measurement past its limit takes points off a starting score of 100, so any single problem moves you out of focused.
  4. One check looks back over time. A yawn counts when your mouth stays wide open for a second, and two yawns within a minute take off another 20 points, even between yawns.
  5. The score sets the state: above 80 is focused, above 60 attentive, above 40 low attention, above 20 distracted, otherwise critical.

Limits

These are hand-written rules, not a trained model. Lighting, glasses and camera angle all move the landmarks. The slouch limit was set by measuring the example photos; the others are starting values, and none have been tuned across many real people. It measures where your head, eyes and shoulders are, not what you're thinking about.
