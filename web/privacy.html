Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short

  • The camera turns on only when you press Start camera, and your browser asks you first.
  • Your video is analysed on your own device. It is never uploaded, recorded or saved.
  • There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • To work, the page loads fonts and the detection models from a few outside services, which see your IP address the way any website does. They never receive your video.

Who runs this site

Focus is a personal portfolio project by Tanishk Jain, hosted at ai-attention-system.vercel.app. It is not a commercial service. For any question about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Your camera and video

  • The site asks for camera access only after you press Start camera. Your browser shows its own permission prompt, and you can say no.
  • Each video frame is processed inside your browser tab by Google's MediaPipe face and pose models, which run on your device. The page turns each frame into a set of points and a score, shows the score, and moves on.
  • No video, image, frame or landmark data is sent anywhere, recorded or stored. There is no server that could receive it: the site is static files only.
  • The score history on screen (the last minute's line and the time in each state) lives only in the open tab and is gone when you close or reload it.
  • The camera turns off when you press Stop camera, close the tab or leave the page. It never runs in the background.
  • The microphone is never requested.

Information we collect

None. The site has no accounts or forms, sets no cookies, writes nothing to your browser's storage, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Outside services the page uses

Like most websites, the page loads some files from other services. Each of them receives the usual details of a web request, such as your IP address, browser type and the time, under its own privacy policy. None of them receives your camera video.

Turning camera access off

If you allowed the camera and want to undo it, click the camera or lock icon in your browser's address bar and set Camera to Block or Ask. You can also find it under Chrome's Settings, Privacy and security, Site settings, Camera; in Safari under Settings for This Website; or in Firefox under the padlock's Connection secure, More information, Permissions.

Children

The site is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU's General Data Protection Regulation give you rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. This site holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access or delete here. For the request details kept by Vercel, Google or jsDelivr, contact those services through the policies linked above.
